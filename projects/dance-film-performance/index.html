D.F.P FESTIVAL

Production Coordinator · Dance. Film. Performance · Copeland Gallery, London

A multidisciplinary event bringing together dance, film, and immersive media, presenting installation works alongside a curated screening programme.

The festival was creatively led by Angelina Gorgaeva, founder of Dance. Film. Performance, and conceptually directed and executed by Alexia Kalogiannidis.

ROLE

Production Coordinator

SCOPE

  • 3-day public event
  • 1-night screening, awards, DJ set, and networking
  • ~150 attendees
  • 4 installation works
  • Team of 6 volunteers

PRODUCTION OVERVIEW

  • Coordinated delivery of a 3-day event programme, including exhibition, screening, and closing night
  • Managed communication with artists to collect films, installation requirements, and technical specifications
  • Structured the awards screening night, integrating film programme, DJ set, and audience flow
  • Supervised and coordinated a team of 6 volunteers across event operations
  • Supported installation of 4 exhibition works within a multi-screen gallery setup
  • Prepared production materials including programme content, print assets, and event documentation

CHALLENGES

  • Integrating multiple formats (installation, screening, live event) into one cohesive programme
  • Managing transitions between exhibition, screening, and social event formats
  • Coordinating team and logistics within a live public environment

OUTCOME

  • Successfully delivered a 3-day public event with ~150 attendees
  • Enabled smooth execution of a multi-phase programme combining exhibition, awards screening, and networking
  • Contributed to a structured and engaging audience experience across all event formats

TOOLS & SKILLS

  • Production coordination & scheduling
  • Team management (volunteers)
  • Artist liaison & deliverables tracking
  • Event logistics & installation support
  • Adobe Suite (print & visual materials)
  • On-site coordination
