R.E.S.T.A.G.E COLLECTIVE

Founder & Creative Producer · Copeland Gallery · 4th–9th February 2026

Conceived, produced and curated the exhibition Choreo-Graphic Systems: Bodies, Tech and Justice at Copeland Gallery (London, February 2026), bringing together artists from performance, moving image, installation and computational practices.

Led the project end-to-end — from curatorial concept and artist selection to production, logistics and on-site delivery — shaping a multi-day public programme including live performances, installations, workshops, talks and a live coding/dance session.

Worked closely with artists and collaborators to develop and present work that explores embodiment, digital systems and social structures, while building partnerships, securing support and managing the operational framework of the exhibition.

ROLE

  • Curatorial concept and artist selection
  • End-to-end production — budgeting, scheduling, logistics and on-site delivery
  • Artist coordination and collaboration
  • Partnership building and securing support
  • Spatial setup, deliveries and equipment coordination
  • Production documents, schedules and references
  • Central point of communication between collaborators and gallery

PROGRAMME

Multi-day public programme at Copeland Gallery featuring live performances, installations, workshops, panel discussions, talks and a live coding/dance session.
