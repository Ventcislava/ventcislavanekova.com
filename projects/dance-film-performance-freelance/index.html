DANCE. FILM. PERFORMANCE

Freelance Production Coordinator · Various locations, London

Freelance production coordination for Dance. Film. Performance — an award-winning contemporary dance company and production agency renowned for seamlessly integrating new media, visual arts, and fashion.

I supported the delivery of their live performance series across various London venues, including Big Sky Studios and St John Church. Each event presented its own technical and spatial requirements — from large-scale projection setups in industrial studios to intimate performances in historic church spaces.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Space planning across multiple venues — designing audience flow, performance areas, and technical zones
  • Logistics — equipment delivery, catering, on-site preparation, and venue liaison
  • Volunteer management — recruiting, briefing, and supervising production volunteers
  • Schedule management — coordinating dancers, performers, and technical crew
  • Guest and crew communication — maintaining clear lines of contact before and during events
  • On-site problem solving — adapting under pressure to last-minute changes
  • Anticipating team needs — proactively supporting performers and crew before issues arose
