About

I'm an emerging creative producer and digital storyteller working at the intersection of performance, technology, and immersive experiences. My background is in design and movement-led projects, and I care deeply about how people experience a space emotionally, physically, and sensorially.

I'm drawn to collaborative environments where ideas evolve quickly and I enjoy being fully embedded in the process from early concept to final delivery, working closely with others to bring ambitious ideas to life.

I'm currently part of the Women@Dior & UNESCO programme, where I'm exploring creative leadership and the role of luxury brands in shaping meaningful experiences.
