ABOUT

Hello! I am Ventcislava and I am a creative producer and a digital storyteller working at the intersection of performance and immersive installation. My background is in design and movement-led projects, and I care deeply about how people experience a space emotionally, physically, and sensorially.

I'm drawn to collaborative environments where ideas evolve quickly and I enjoy being fully embedded in the process from early concept to final delivery, working closely with others to bring ambitious ideas to life.

I'm currently developing R.E.S.T.A.G.E, a female led visual arts and dance centred collective, where I'm exploring creative leadership in shaping meaningful experiences.
